## Subscription Data

When you subscribe to FitScan Pro, your subscription is processed through Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat as our subscription management platform. RevenueCat receives your Google Play purchase token and subscription status to manage your entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Your subscription status (active, trialing, expired) is mirrored on our server to manage access to premium features.

## Device Identifier

We generate a random device identifier (UUID) on first app launch, stored locally on your device. This identifier is sent to our server with each scan request to track monthly usage for our free tier limits. This identifier is not linked to your Google account, name, email, or any personally identifiable information. It resets if you reinstall the app.

## Analytics Events

We collect anonymous usage events to understand how our app is used and improve the experience. These events include: quiz completion, scan attempts, paywall interactions, and subscription events. Each event is associated only with your device identifier (not your name or email). Events are stored on our server and are not shared with third parties.

## RevenueCat as Processor

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a data processor for subscription management. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. RevenueCat processes your subscription data on our behalf and does not use it for their own purposes.
